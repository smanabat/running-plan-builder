# DECISION-0001: Build an explainable running-plan MVP

- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Owners:** Project owner

## Context

The existing personal marathon plan is an example built from running history, a race goal and calendar constraints. The project should grow into a useful application. The first product direction needs to remain small enough to build and evaluate.

## Decision

Explore personalized, explainable training plans for recreational road runners. Start with marathon planning, CSV-based activity import, runner preferences, deterministic versioned planning rules, visible rationale and user-reviewed plan adjustments. Treat the initial audience and unmet need as hypotheses to validate, not established market facts.

## Consequences

The first version emphasizes trust, understandable planning and a complete local workflow over broad integrations, automatic adaptation or monetization. The portfolio story will document product discovery and decision evidence alongside implementation.

## Review trigger

Revisit after discovery interviews and early usability sessions, or if qualified coaching review changes the product's safe scope.
